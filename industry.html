Professional principles

Editorial independence stays intact.

Sponsorship can support distribution and development, but it does not buy control of safety advice, legal guidance or editorial conclusions.

HGV Driving Explained is practical guidance and does not replace formal training, current legislation, DVSA/GOV.UK guidance, an employer's safe system of work or manufacturer instructions.
